Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

We deliver within 30-45 minutes in south Kolkata. Same-day delivery available for orders before 2 PM.

Are your custards made fresh?

Yes! All custards are made fresh daily in the morning. We don't store or freeze them.

Do you use artificial flavors?

No! We use 100% natural ingredients. No artificial colors or flavors added.

Can I customize flavors for bulk orders?

Absolutely! We can create custom recipes for weddings and corporate events.

What's your delivery area?

We deliver across south Kolkata. Minimum order ₹200. Extra charges apply for areas beyond 10km.

Can I pre-order for future dates?

Yes! Pre-orders are available for 30+ orders. Contact us for bulk bookings.
